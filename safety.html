Safety & care

A place you can leave your child without worrying

Everything listed here exists at our school today — nothing more, nothing less.

5 large separate classrooms

Every age group has its own big classroom with wide, comfortable seating — no crowding, no shared corners.

5500 sq ft campus

2500 sq ft of classrooms plus a 3000 sq ft private playground used only by our children, for daily outdoor play, activities and school events.

Full CCTV coverage

The complete school area is under CCTV surveillance throughout school hours.

Digital learning setup

Projector, TV and large speakers used for stories, rhymes, themes and activity sessions.

Government registered

First Step Preschool is a government-registered preschool run by Shri Swami Samarth Krupa Foundation.

Van service available

Safe van service is available for our students across nearby areas of Satpur, Nashik.

Professional, friendly staff

Teachers who are patient and warm with children, and easy for parents to talk to.

Yearly gathering & functions

Annual gathering and social functions where every child gets a chance on stage.
