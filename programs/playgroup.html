Playgroup

The gentlest possible start. Playgroup is about a child feeling safe away from home for the first time — learning that school is a warm, happy place.

What we focus on

  • Settling in and separating happily from parents
  • Talking, listening and naming the world around them
  • Sensory play — sand, water, dough, colour, texture
  • Running, climbing, balancing on our own playground
  • Simple routines: circle time, snack time, tidy-up time

A typical day

  1. 1.Welcome and circle time with songs
  2. 2.Free play and sensory activity
  3. 3.Rhymes, stories and picture talk
  4. 4.Snack time and washing hands independently
  5. 5.Outdoor play on the school playground

By the end of Playgroup

A child who walks into school smiling, speaks more words each week, and enjoys being with other children.
