Senior KG

The bridge to big school. Senior KG prepares a child academically, socially and emotionally for Standard 1 — so the change feels exciting, not frightening.

What we focus on

  • Reading readiness — phonics, sight words and simple reading
  • Writing readiness — letters, numbers and words
  • Early maths — counting, addition, patterns and shapes
  • Confidence — speaking in front of others, asking and answering
  • School habits — sitting, listening, doing homework and self-help

A typical day

  1. 1.Circle time — calendar, counting and conversation
  2. 2.English, phonics and writing practice
  3. 3.Maths through games, objects and worksheets
  4. 4.EVS, stories, rhymes and show-and-tell
  5. 5.Outdoor play, music and group games

By the end of Senior KG

A child who enters Standard 1 with early reading and writing skills, steady concentration and the confidence to raise their hand.
