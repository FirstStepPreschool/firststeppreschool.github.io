Junior KG

The year skills come together. Junior KG turns play into early reading, early writing and early thinking — without ever losing the play.

What we focus on

  • Early phonics, letter recognition and pre-writing
  • Counting to 20 and beyond, with real objects
  • Listening, speaking and expressing ideas clearly
  • Pencil grip, cutting, pasting and fine motor skills
  • Following instructions, waiting for turns and working in groups

A typical day

  1. 1.Circle time with days, numbers and rhymes
  2. 2.Phonics and early writing practice
  3. 3.Theme activity — art, craft or table work
  4. 4.Story, music and movement
  5. 5.Outdoor play and free choice

By the end of Junior KG

A child who recognises letters and numbers, holds a pencil steadily, listens well and works happily with friends.
