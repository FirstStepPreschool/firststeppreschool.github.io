Junior KG

The year skills come together. Junior KG turns play into early reading, early writing and early thinking — without ever losing the play.

What we focus on

  • Phonic sounds and blending simple words
  • Pattern writing and formation of letters and numbers
  • Number concepts, comparison and simple addition ideas
  • Observing, questioning, reasoning and problem solving
  • Confidence on stage — speaking, acting and performing

A typical day

  1. 1.Circle time and news sharing
  2. 2.Phonics and language work
  3. 3.Number work with concrete material
  4. 4.Digital learning session on projector and TV
  5. 5.Creative activity, then outdoor play

By the end of Junior KG

A child who recognises sounds and numbers, writes with control, and speaks in front of the class without fear.
