About us

A small school with big intentions

Shri Swami Samarth Krupa Foundation's First Step Preschool — established 2024.

Our story

First Step Preschool began in 2024 as part of the work of Shri Swami Samarth Krupa Foundation — an organisation built around one simple belief: that no family should have to struggle alone at the beginning of life's most important moments.

The Foundation's work supports newborns, families, women and community health. First Step is its education arm — the answer to a question parents kept asking: where can our child get a real preschool education, in a safe place, close to home, without paying franchise fees?

Today 101+ children come through our gates every morning. They are taught across 2500 sq ft of large, separate classrooms, they play on a 3000 sq ft playground that belongs only to them, and they go home to parents who know exactly what happened during the day.

Our vision

That every child in our community begins school confident, curious and cared for — and that their first classroom becomes a happy memory, not a hurdle.

Our mission

To give children aged 2 to 6 a safe, joyful and structured early education that develops the whole child, and to keep parents genuine partners in that journey.

What we stand for

Warmth first

A child who feels loved learns faster. Kindness is not a bonus at First Step — it is the method.

Honesty with parents

We tell you exactly what your child did today, what they enjoyed and where they need support. No exaggeration.

Learning through doing

Children at this age learn with their hands, feet and voices — not by sitting still and copying.

Every child, individually

No two children develop at the same speed. We plan for the child in front of us, not for an average.

Values and good habits

Respect, sharing, cleanliness, gratitude and courage are taught daily, alongside letters and numbers.

Accessible to our community

Standardised, affordable education for the families around us — quality should not be a luxury.

Part of the Foundation's wider work

First Step Preschool sits alongside the Foundation's other initiatives in newborn support, community healthcare and family wellbeing. Being part of a foundation — not a franchise — is why our fees stay standardised and our focus stays on the child.
