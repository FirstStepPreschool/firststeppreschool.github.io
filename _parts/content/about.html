About us

A small school with big intentions

Shri Swami Samarth Krupa Foundation's First Step Preschool — established 2024.

Our story

First Step Preschool opened in 2024 as the education arm of Shri Swami Samarth Krupa Foundation — an organisation already supporting newborns, families and community health in Nashik.

Parents kept asking for a real preschool — safe, close to home and without franchise fees. Today 101+ children learn here every morning: in large separate classrooms across 2500 sq ft, on a private 3000 sq ft playground, and with parents who know exactly what their child did each day.

Our vision

That every child in our community begins school confident, curious and cared for — and that their first classroom becomes a happy memory.

Our mission

To give children aged 2 to 6 a safe, joyful and structured early education that develops the whole child, with parents as genuine partners.

What we stand for

Warmth first

A child who feels loved learns faster. Kindness is the method.

Honesty with parents

We tell you exactly what your child did today — no exaggeration.

Learning through doing

Children learn with their hands, feet and voices — not by sitting still.

Every child, individually

We plan for the child in front of us, not for an average.

Values and good habits

Respect, sharing and gratitude are taught daily, alongside letters and numbers.

Accessible to our community

Standardised, affordable education — quality should not be a luxury.

Part of the Foundation's wider work

First Step sits alongside the Foundation's newborn support, community healthcare and family wellbeing work. Being part of a foundation — not a franchise — is why our fees stay standardised and our focus stays on the child.
