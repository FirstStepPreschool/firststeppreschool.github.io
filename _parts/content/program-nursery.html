Nursery

Curiosity year. Children ask a hundred questions a day, and Nursery is built around answering them through doing rather than telling.

What we focus on

  • Answering a hundred questions a day through doing
  • Vocabulary, rhymes, stories and conversation
  • Early sorting, counting and colour recognition
  • Big-muscle movement, balance and coordination
  • Making friends, sharing and following simple routines

A typical day

  1. 1.Circle time with songs, weather and date talk
  2. 2.Theme-based activity — art, craft or sensory
  3. 3.Storytelling, rhymes and picture talk
  4. 4.Snack time and tidy-up
  5. 5.Outdoor play on the school playground

By the end of Nursery

A child who asks questions confidently, speaks in small sentences, and begins to recognise numbers, colours and shapes.
