Nursery

Curiosity year. Children ask a hundred questions a day, and Nursery is built around answering them through doing rather than telling.

What we focus on

  • Vocabulary, conversation and confident speaking
  • Pre-literacy: sounds, rhymes, picture reading
  • Early number sense — counting, sorting, matching, shapes
  • Colouring, threading, tearing, pasting for finger strength
  • Sharing, waiting for a turn and playing in a group

A typical day

  1. 1.Circle time, calendar, weather and greeting
  2. 2.Theme activity on the projector and speaker system
  3. 3.Language or number activity at the table
  4. 4.Art, craft, music or movement
  5. 5.Outdoor play and closing rhyme

By the end of Nursery

A child who speaks in full sentences, counts with meaning, and holds a crayon with control.
