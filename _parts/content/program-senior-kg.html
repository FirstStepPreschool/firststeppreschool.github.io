Senior KG

The bridge to big school. Senior KG prepares a child academically, socially and emotionally for Standard 1 — so the change feels exciting, not frightening.

What we focus on

  • Reading readiness — blending, sight words, simple sentences
  • Writing readiness — words, sentences, neatness, speed
  • Numeracy: number sense, addition, subtraction, measurement ideas
  • Logical thinking, memory games and attention span
  • Independence, responsibility and following instructions

A typical day

  1. 1.Circle time and thought for the day
  2. 2.Language: reading and writing block
  3. 3.Mathematics block with activities
  4. 4.Theme, general awareness or values session
  5. 5.Creative or sports activity, then closing

By the end of Senior KG

A child who is genuinely ready for Standard 1 — able to read simple text, write, sit through a lesson and manage themselves.
