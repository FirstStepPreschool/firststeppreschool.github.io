The First Step Growth Framework

We don't just teach subjects.We grow children.

Six areas of development that guide everything we plan, every single day.

01

Language & Communication

Listening, speaking, expressing

Between 2 and 6, a child moves from single words to full conversations. This is the base every other subject stands on.

02

Early Numeracy & Thinking

Counting, patterns, reasoning

Numbers become real when children touch, sort and count actual things — not when they memorise them.

03

Body & Movement

Gross motor, fine motor, balance

Strong shoulders and steady fingers come before neat handwriting. Physical play is academic preparation.

04

Social Skills & Friendship

Sharing, cooperation, belonging

School is often a child's first community. Learning to share and take turns is a lifelong skill learnt here.

05

Feelings & Confidence

Emotions, self-regulation, courage

A child who can name what they feel is far calmer than a child who cannot. Confidence grows from being heard.

06

Creativity, Values & Independence

Imagination, character, self-help

Imagination and good habits are formed early. So is the pride of doing something without help.
