First Step Moments

A journal of our school events

Photos and memories from celebrations, activities and everyday life at First Step Preschool.

15 Dec 2025

Annual Day Celebration

Children performed dances, skits and songs at the Annual Day celebration. Parents joined us for a wonderful evening of performances and awards.

26 Jan 2026

Republic Day Celebration

Students participated in flag hoisting and patriotic songs. Children dressed in tri-colour outfits and performed a special assembly.

10 Feb 2026

Sports Day

A fun-filled sports day with relay races, ball games and obstacle courses. Every child participated and received a medal for their enthusiasm.

20 Dec 2025

Christmas Celebration

Children decorated the Christmas tree, made paper stars and performed carols. Santa visited the school with gifts for everyone.

5 Mar 2026

Art Exhibition

Students showcased their artwork including paintings, drawings and craft projects. Parents walked through the exhibition and admired the creativity.

Sample events are shown for now. Real photos and event details will be added as they happen.
